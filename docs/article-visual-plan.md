## Asset Guidelines for Publication

1. **Resolution & Quality**: All images are full-resolution 1920x1080 / 16:9 captures.
2. **Privacy Compliance**: All screenshots use sanitized demonstration profiles (e.g. Alex Chen sample profile) with zero private personal identifiable information (PII).
3. **Security Compliance**: No AWS credentials, internal account IDs, session tokens, or private endpoints are visible in any graphic.
